02

Scope of work

Website — General

₹18,000 · core
  • Custom design in the firm's branding; mobile, tablet & desktop ready
  • Up to 6 pages — Home, About the Firm, Practice Areas, Our Team, Insights, Contact
  • Bar Council of India disclaimer (Rule 36) shown on entry
  • Enquiry form with email alerts, click-to-call, WhatsApp & Google Map
  • SSL (https), speed optimisation & basic security
  • 2 rounds of design revisions, handover & 30 days post-launch support

Standard SEO — 2 weeks

Included with the website
  • Page titles, meta descriptions, headings & image alt-text on all pages
  • XML sitemap, robots.txt & Google indexing request
  • Google Search Console + Google Analytics 4 setup
  • Google Business Profile (Maps listing) setup & optimisation
  • Law-firm schema markup for richer search results
  • Closing report with baseline traffic & rankings

Full Professional SEO — +6 weeks

₹8,000 · optional add-on
  • Keyword research & competitor analysis for each practice area and city
  • Technical SEO audit & fixes — speed, Core Web Vitals, crawl errors
  • Local SEO — weekly Google Business Profile posts + up to 15 legal & local directory listings
  • Up to 3 SEO-optimised articles on topics approved by the firm
  • Internal linking & on-page content improvements
  • Weekly progress updates + final ranking & traffic report
All SEO work stays within Bar Council of India norms — informational content only, no solicitation. Search rankings depend on Google and cannot be guaranteed; results typically build over 3–6 months.
